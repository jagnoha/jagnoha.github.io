  • Channel-specific behaviorThe multichannel and publishing applications had to present common operational workflows across distinct sales channels.
  • Product synchronizationThe documented integration connected vendor and Flxpoint data with four external commerce destinations at meaningful catalog scale.
  • Operational usabilityMobile publishing and centralized order tooling moved repeat commerce work into interfaces built for marketing and operations users.
  • Pricing configurabilityThe eBay application separated competitive inputs from configurable pricing rules; deeper implementation details are not retained.
