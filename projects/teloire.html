01 / System principle

Canonical data, derived views.

Teloire separates durable business facts from projections, sessions, provider responses, and UI state. PostgreSQL owns listings, availability, reservations, quotes, orders, and financial records; search projections and Redis-backed controls can be rebuilt or lost without redefining commerce truth.

The repository contains broad buyer and seller functionality. This case study focuses on two implemented workflows where those boundaries are easiest to inspect: product discovery and checkout.

02 / Implemented workflow one

From listing event to stable search session.

Search stays useful without requiring a remote AI call, and explicit constraints remain eligibility gates rather than ranking hints.

  1. 01

    Canonical listing mutation

    Seller-owned Item and Listing records pass publication gates for eligibility, price, size, condition, and ready media.

  2. 02

    Transactional outbox fact

    The listing change and its durable event commit together. Workers claim rows with PostgreSQL locking and leases.

  3. 03

    Disposable search projection

    A consumer builds a version-aware read model; delayed events cannot overwrite newer listing state.

  4. 04

    Hard filters, then ranking

    PostgreSQL FTS/trigram retrieval and optional, provider-bounded semantic channels union candidates, reapply eligibility, and deduplicate by Item.

  5. 05

    Immutable private session

    Resolved intent and ordered results are snapshotted so cursor pages do not reparse or reorder the query.

04 / Evidence and status

Implemented, experimental, planned.

The repository retains unit/integration coverage, responsive browser suites, security checks, and provider sandbox records. These are implementation signals, not evidence of a launched service.

ImplementedCanonical listings, outbox processing, deterministic search and private sessions, reservation concurrency, authoritative quotes, and idempotent order finalization
ExperimentalOptional provider-bounded AI enrichment and semantic retrieval, with deterministic fallback; provider integrations remain sandbox or opt-in
Planned / launch-blockedProduction provider activation, managed infrastructure, monitoring, backup/PITR, and outstanding business, legal, and tax decisions

Pre-launch development status

Teloire is not publicly accessible, and no product link is published here. Production readiness remains in progress. No claim is made about launch, users, revenue, traffic, or production capacity; local and sandbox validation should not be read as production evidence.
